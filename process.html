Our Process

How We
Work

Six clear steps from the first conversation to the final walkthrough. No surprises along the way.

01

Initial Consultation

Every project starts with a conversation. We meet at your home to discuss goals, assess the space, and talk budget and timeline.

02

Design & Planning

Design plans, 3D renderings and material samples, plus an itemized estimate and contract, so you see exactly what you're getting.

03

Permits & Prep

We handle the paperwork, coordinate utilities, order materials and protect your home's work areas.

04

Construction

We manage subcontractors, clean up daily, run quality checks and keep you updated as the work moves along.

05

Inspection & Completion

Required inspections, an internal quality review, punch-list items and a final cleaning.

06

Final Walkthrough

The moment you've been waiting for. We walk through it together and cover systems, warranty and care tips.
